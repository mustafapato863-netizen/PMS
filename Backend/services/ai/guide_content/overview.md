# PMS System Overview

## PMS System Overview

The Performance Management System (**PMS**) tracks verified monthly KPI performance across Egypt and UAE teams at the **Employee**, **Managerial**, and **Corporate** levels.

- **Executive Summary** (`/executive`, formerly Monthly Review): unified executive monthly overview with official team scores, Region Performance Share, Grade Distribution, Key Movers, Functions Overview, Teams at Risk, department/team deep dives, branch breakdowns, and Presentation Mode.
- **Executive Insights** (`/insights`): cross-team KPI diagnosis, score drivers, trends, and risk indicators for your authorized scope.
- **Team Dashboard** (`/team/<team-slug>`): team-level employee scorecards, weighted KPI achievement, grade distribution, and 6-month trends.
- **Corrective Actions** (`/corrective-actions`): track **All actions** and switch to **Follow-up** to monitor **Overdue**, **Due this week**, **Upcoming**, and **Completed** actions.
- **PMS AI Assistant**: ask read-only questions about verified scores, trends, rankings, corrective actions, or where to find screens in PMS.
