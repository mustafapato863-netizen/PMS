# Executive Summary Guide

## Executive Summary and Monthly Review Overview

**Executive Summary** (`/executive`) is PMS's unified leadership overview and monthly review workspace (it consolidates the former Monthly Review / Monthly Presentation and replaces the hidden legacy Executive Summary view; legacy `/monthly-presentation` links redirect automatically to `/executive`):

- **Executive Summary** (`/executive`): Review verified monthly performance with Region (`All regions`, `Offshore Egypt`, `UAE Region`) and Branch filters, Overall Performance & Gap to `100%`, 6-Month Score Trend, Region Performance Share, Grade Distribution, Key Movers, Functions Overview, Teams at Risk, Corrective Actions, department/team/role deep dives, and Presentation Mode.
- **Key Movers (What Moved the Score)**: Highlights the Top Negative Drivers and Top Positive Drivers by weighted impact. Movement arrows are direction-aware—green means the metric moved in the favorable direction (for example, Quality Errors Rate going down), and red means it worsened.

## Teams at Risk and Watchlist Status Badges

The **Teams at Risk** table in **Executive Summary** ranks underperforming teams worst-first with six executive columns:

- **Team & Scope**: Team name along with its parent Function, Region (`EGY` / `UAE`), and evaluated headcount.
- **Score & Grade**: Weighted team score and official PMS grade badge (`Grade A`–`Grade E`).
- **Gap Share**: Percentage of the overall company gap driven by that team (headcount-weighted).
- **Primary Bottleneck KPI**: The single KPI contributing the largest weighted gap, with actual vs target values.
- **Status Badges**: **Critical <70%** means the team scored **Grade E** (below `70%`); **Falling 2 mo** means the team's score declined for two consecutive months; **Below 90%** means the team is below the `90%` Meet Expectations benchmark (**Grade C** or **Grade D**).
- **Review Team**: Opens the team's deep-dive scorecard and KPI breakdown within your authorized scope.
