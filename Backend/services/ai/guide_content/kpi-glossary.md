# PMS KPI Glossary

## Common PMS KPIs and Metric Directions

Each KPI in PMS has a configured direction (**Higher is better** or **Lower is better**) and a unit (`%`, `count`, `min`/`sec`, or currency `AED`/`EGP`):

- **Call Center (Inbound & Outbound)**: **Reachability** (higher better), **Utilization** (higher better), **Booking Rate** (higher better), **Patient Attendance Rate** (higher better), **Quality / QA Score** (higher better), **AHT / Average Handle Time** (lower better), and **Abandonment Rate** (lower better).
- **RCM & Pre-Approvals**: **TAT Compliance %** (higher better), **Productivity / Target Achievement** (higher better), **Quality Errors Rate** (lower better), **Controllable Rejection Rate** (lower better), **Policy Rejection Rate** (lower better), and **Rework Rate** (lower better).
- **Clinical, Pharmacy, Sales & Marketing**: **OP / IP Revenue Achievement** (higher better, displayed in `AED` or `EGP`), **Patient Waiting Time** (lower better, in minutes), **Leads / Conversion** (higher better), **CPL / Cost Per Lead** (lower better), and **Response Time** (lower better).
- **0%-Weight Diagnostic KPIs**: Some metrics are tracked as diagnostic context (`0%` weight) so teams can monitor volume or operational signals without altering the official weighted PMS score.
