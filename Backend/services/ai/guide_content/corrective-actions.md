# Corrective Actions Guide

## Finding Corrective Actions

You can view and manage corrective actions on the **Corrective Actions** page (`/corrective-actions`).

- The page opens on the **All actions** tab by default, listing actions across your authorized scope.
- To focus on due dates and follow-up tracking, select the **Follow-up** tab at the top of the page.
- Use the **All teams**, **All months**, and **All types** filters or the search bar to narrow the list to a specific employee, team, or reporting month.

## Following Up on Overdue Actions

Follow these steps to review and follow up on overdue corrective actions:

1. Open **Corrective Actions** (`/corrective-actions`).
2. Switch from the default **All actions** tab to the **Follow-up** tab.
3. Open the state dropdown (**All states**) and choose **Overdue**.
4. Review the overdue items by team, owner, and due date, then update status or add a completion note if your role has write permission.

An action is classified as **Overdue** when its status is `Open` or `In Progress` and its due date is earlier than the PMS reference date.

## Filtering Corrective Actions by State and Team

Inside **Corrective Actions → Follow-up**, you can filter tracked items using the state selector and scope dropdowns:

- **State filter (`All states`)**: **Overdue**, **Due this week**, **Upcoming**, **No due date**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled**.
- **Scope filters**: filter by team, owner, or reporting month within your authorized PMS access scope.
- Switching between **Overdue** and **Completed** updates the displayed follow-up queue without changing your selected team filter.
