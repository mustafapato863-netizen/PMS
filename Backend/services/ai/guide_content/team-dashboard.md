# Team Dashboard Navigation

## Checking a Specific Team

To inspect performance for a specific team in PMS:

1. Open **Executive Summary** (`/executive`) or **Executive Insights** (`/insights`), or choose the team directly from the navigation menu.
2. Select the target **Function / Department** and **Team** (for example, Inbound, Outbound, Marketing, Pharmacy, or Human Resources) within your authorized access scope.
3. Choose the reporting **Month**, **Year**, and **Performance Level** (`Employee`, `Managerial`, or `Corporate`).
4. Open the team's **Team Dashboard** (`/team/<team-slug>`) to inspect overall score, weighted KPI actuals vs targets, 6-month trajectory, and individual employee scorecards.

If you name a specific authorized team (for example, "How can I check Outbound?"), PMS can provide a direct link to that team's dashboard.
