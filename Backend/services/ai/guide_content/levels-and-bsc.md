# Performance Levels and Balanced Scorecard Guide

## Performance Levels (Employee, Managerial, Corporate)

PMS evaluates performance across three distinct organizational levels, all using the same **A–E** grade scale:

- **Employee Level**: Individual agent and specialist monthly scorecards measured against team-specific KPI targets and weights (for example, Call Center agents, RCM coders, Marketing specialists, Pharmacists, and Sales reps).
- **Managerial Level**: Team leaders, supervisors, and operational managers evaluated on Managerial Balanced Scorecards covering team attainment, SLAs, quality governance, and coaching execution.
- **Corporate Level**: Department and group leadership scorecards (such as RCM, Nursing, PMO, CRM, Finance, HR, and IT) measured against strategic organizational KPIs.
- **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)** (`/executive`): Organizes Managerial and Corporate KPIs into strategic perspectives (**Financial**, **Customer**, **Internal Process**, and **Learning & Growth**) with perspective weights and KPI weights totaling `100%`.
